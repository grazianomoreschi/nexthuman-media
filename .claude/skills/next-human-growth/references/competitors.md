## 12 patterns in the most viral videos
1. "What happens to your body if/when..." with escalating milestones (1 h, 1 day, 1 week).
2. "You" as the protagonist.
3. Myth-bust openers ("Does X really...?").
4. Mild body horror and disgust (parasites, pimples, black lungs): drives shares and comments.
5. Survival limits (no air, water, food; space; altitude; heat; cold).
6. Death and after-death.
7. Everyday action with a hidden consequence (sneezing, knuckles, soda).
8. A giant number that climbs on screen (lifetime heartbeats) - fits our timer.
9. World-scale "What if".
10. Immune system and cells as a war or characters.
11. A real person / real case as the hook - strongest hook type.
12. Packaging: 20-45 s, new visual every 2-3 s, payoff at the end, loop to the start, recurring series and
    authority cues (a tiny source line on screen).

Key difference: almost all of them use a voiceover. Without voice, our on-screen text must do the narrator's
job: huge type, 3-6 words per beat, a new beat every 2-3 s, first line on screen at 0 s.

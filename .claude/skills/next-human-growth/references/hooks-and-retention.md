# Hooks and retention for Next Human timer reels

Adapted from vyralcontent/content-skills (`viral-hooks`, `viral-instagram-reels/references/reels-hook.md`,
`viral-short-form/references/retention.md`, MIT) and fitted to our format: pixel-art scene, big timer box,
words popping in, no voice, chiptune.

## The 3-second cliff
Instagram decides in the first ~3 s whether to keep testing a reel (that is what `reels_skip_rate` measures).
- Frame 1 already shows the subject AND the claim: the scene, the big number, the first caption words. No
  title-only frame, no subtitle intro, no empty background, no slow zoom.
- Sound-off: the hook must read on screen in 3-8 words, high contrast, outside the top and bottom fifth.
- Movement in the first second (the scene animates, a number counts, a punch-in).
- The first frame doubles as the cover: compose it like a paused moment ("click to unpause").

## Hook archetypes that fit us (pick one per reel, vary across a day)
- Named viewer / you-situation: "At hour 17, your brain drives like it's drunk." (best skip so far: 31%)
- Shocking true number: "1 in 3 adults..." Odd specific numbers read as measured.
- Investigator: "Scientists found a switch in your brain that..."
- Contrarian: "Everyone says 10,000 steps. Your brain says 3,000." The payoff must defend the claim.
- Experimenter / timeline: "What 24 hours without water does to you."
- Magician (visual first): the scene shows the surprise, the text points at it.
Avoid: vague openers ("This is your brain"), jargon or brand-first lines ("This weekly shot"), questions that
can be answered "no".

## Retention structure (timer format)
Hook (0-2 s) -> escalation beats (each raises the stakes: bigger number, worse/better state) -> re-hook
("but here's the catch") at about 40-50% of the reel -> payoff -> CTA (own beat, 2-3 s).
- Join beats with "but / so", never "and then".
- A change every 2-3 s: timer tick, new scene, punch-in, counter.
- Put the second-best fact near the top, the best at the payoff.
- Short reel first: with 11-12 s average watch, aim at 20-25 s until watch % beats baseline (playbook rule).
- Loop: a last line or frame that makes the first frame hit again drives rewatches.

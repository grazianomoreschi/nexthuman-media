# Sends, saves, captions, pinned comment

Adapted from vyralcontent/content-skills `viral-instagram-reels/references/sends-playbook.md` and
`caption-and-search.md` (MIT).

## Why sends
Instagram has said sends (DM shares) per reach is a top signal for reaching non-followers; a send weighs
several times a like. Saves say "useful". Likes mostly re-reach followers. Design every reel for a send.

## Payoff shapes that earn sends (pick one)
1. Named-persona favour: "Send this to the friend who sleeps 5 hours and says they're fine."
2. Receipt for an argument: the reel settles a common myth ("10,000 steps", "fish oil").
3. Tagged callout: "POV: your friend who drinks 4 coffees before noon."
4. Named list: "the 3 signs of a stroke: F.A.S.T." A name people can repeat in a DM.
5. Safety favour: "Send this to someone who swims in cold water."

## CTA rules
- The CTA names the recipient: "Send this to the friend who ...". Generic "comment / like / follow for more"
  reads as engagement bait and wastes the last beat.
- One ask per reel. On screen as its own beat.
- Alternate send-CTAs with save-CTAs on list/practical reels ("Save this for Sunday").

## Caption
- First line = the hook with the topic keyword people would search ("sleep deprivation", "stroke signs").
- 150-300 characters before the hashtags is the working range; the timeline lines are fine below.
- 3-5 relevant hashtags, always #nexthuman. Source line in plain names.
- Pinned comment: the honest caveat or a question that invites a reply, plus the next reel teaser.

# Virality research behind the playbook (as of 2026-10-23)

Sourced through web search summaries (full texts not opened in this environment). Platform statements mostly
via trade press. Confidence in brackets.

## Academic
- Berger & Milkman 2012, Journal of Marketing Research (NYT most-emailed): high-arousal emotions (awe,
  anxiety, anger) drive sharing; low-arousal (sadness) suppresses it; practical value and surprise help. [high]
- Tellis et al. 2019, Journal of Marketing: purely informational video reduces sharing; emotion increases it.
  [high]
- Robertson et al. 2023, Nature Human Behaviour (105k Upworthy A/B tests): each negative word +~2.3% CTR,
  positive words ~-1%. Use for framing, keep claims true. [high]
- Loewenstein 1994, Psychological Bulletin: curiosity = a felt, closable information gap; it drops once
  filled. The climbing timer is an information gap only if each step raises the stakes. [high]
- Ghibellini & Meier 2025 meta-analysis: no reliable memory advantage for unfinished tasks (Zeigarnik); the
  pull to resume (Ovsiankina) holds. Open loops work as a pull to completion. [medium]
- Berger, "Contagious" (2013), STEPPS: social currency, triggers, emotion, public, practical value, stories.
  [medium]

## Platforms
- Instagram (Mosseri 2025, secondary): top signals are watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; sends
  matter most for non-followers. First 3 s "super important"; use on-screen text (sound off). Skip rate and
  retention graph added to Insights in 2025. Hashtags do not raise reach, help search. Explicit "comment WORD"
  bait can make a reel ineligible for recommendation; open questions are fine. Watermarked or reposted content
  is demoted; originality enforcement extended. Trial Reels (1,000+ followers) test on non-followers. "3-5x
  sends vs likes" is widely repeated but has no traceable source. [medium-high]
- YouTube Shorts: "viewed vs swiped away" is the hook metric (top Shorts ~70-90% viewed, unofficial). Every
  play counts as a view since 31 Mar 2025. Since 15 Jul 2025 "mass-produced or repetitive" content counts as
  inauthentic for monetisation. [medium]
- TikTok Creative Tips (official PDF): over 63% of top-CTR videos put the key message in the first 3 s.
  [medium-high]
- Facebook/Nielsen (~2016): 47% of a mobile video's value lands in the first 3 s, 74% in the first 10 s.
  [medium]
- Verizon Media/Publicis 2019 (n=5,616): 69% watch with sound off in public. [medium]
- Buffer 2025 (2M posts): more posts per week, more reach per post, with diminishing returns; no data on 7 a
  day. Mosseri: no penalty for several posts a day, quality over volume. [medium]
- Reel length: short reels (7-25 s) complete and share best for new/low-retention accounts; longer reels do
  well only with strong retention (Socialinsider; correlational). [medium]

# Learnings (newest first)

## 2026-10-23 (setup of the growth system)
- 4 Instagram reels: 120-139 views each, reach 106-124, almost all non-followers (account has 1 follower).
- Skip rate (left in the first ~3 s): No Sleep 31%, Quit Smoking 38%, Stroke 50%, Ozempic 51%. The best hook
  was about "you" in a known situation ("At hour 17, your brain drives like it's drunk").
- Average watch 10.6-12.5 s whatever the length (36-44 s): watch 25-32%. The shortest reel had the best %.
- 0 shares, 0 saves, 0 comments, 3 likes in total: viewers leave before the payoff (timer twist after 25-30 s).
- Ozempic made 39 views/h in its first 3 hours, then slowed: Instagram tested it on a small audience and
  stopped after the weak skip/watch signals.
- Opened experiment E1 (format v2: 20-25 s, claim at frame 1, named-persona send CTA). The reels of 24 Oct are
  still v1 (control); v2 starts with the reels of 25 Oct.

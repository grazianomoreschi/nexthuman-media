# Next Human playbook (living rules)

Status: PROVEN (our data confirms it on 3+ reels per variant), TESTING (in an open experiment or applied while
data comes in), IDEA (hypothesis, not applied yet), REJECTED (our data says no). Every change of status gets
the date and the evidence. Updated by the reviews (skill `next-human-growth`).

Baseline on 2026-10-23 (4 Instagram reels, new account, 1 follower): skip 31-51% (median ~44%), watch 25-32%
of 36-44 s (avg 11-12 s), ~0 shares/saves/comments, 120-139 views each.

## Hook and first frame
1. TESTING. Frame 1 already shows the subject, the key claim and the number. No subtitle intro, no title-only
   frame. Evidence: Mosseri, TikTok tips (first 3 s); our reels all had a 4 s intro before the timer and skip
   was 31-51%. Since 2026-10-23.
2. TESTING. Hook about "you" in a recognisable situation beats vague or technical openers. Evidence (ours):
   No Sleep "At hour 17, your brain drives like it's drunk" skip 31%, vs "This is your brain. Right now."
   (Stroke) 50% and "This weekly shot cut 15%..." (Ozempic) 51%. n=4, so TESTING. Since 2026-10-23.
3. IDEA. Open on the end state ("At 72 hours your body starts eating itself"), then rewind the timer to 0.
4. TESTING. Max 8 words of on-screen text per beat, high contrast, away from the top and bottom fifth.

## Length and pacing
5. TESTING. 20-25 s reels (4-6 timer stops) instead of 35-45 s until watch % beats 50%. Evidence: avg watch
   11-12 s on every reel regardless of length; shortest reel (36 s) had the best watch % (32%). Since
   2026-10-23.
6. TESTING. Every stop beats the previous one; best fact second-to-last, twist last; re-hook at ~40-50%.
   (Loewenstein 1994.)
7. TESTING. A change on screen every 1.5-3 s (timer tick, scene, punch-in, counter, sound hit).
8. IDEA. Loop ending: the last frame matches the first so the replay feels seamless.

## Sends, saves, comments
9. TESTING. One send-worthy angle per reel and a named-persona CTA ("Send this to the friend who ...");
   no "comment WORD" bait. (Berger & Milkman 2012; Instagram CTA guidance 2024.)
10. TESTING. Each script has an awe or personal-stakes beat plus one practical takeaway; avoid the neutral
    "science fact" tone (Tellis 2019).
11. IDEA. End question people can actually answer ("Which hour would you quit at?") for comments.

## Topics and format
12. IDEA. A recognisable series name with the same visual grammar (e.g. "YOUR BODY AFTER... #N"), but new
    scenes, palettes and music every time (avoid mass-produced sameness; YouTube policy 2025).
13. TESTING. Vary scenes, palettes and music per reel (already done with themes).
14. IDEA. Topics tied to daily triggers (coffee, phone in bed, gym, alcohol, sugar) so the reel comes back to
    mind (STEPPS: triggers).

## Captions and distribution
15. TESTING. Caption first line = hook with the searchable keyword; 3-5 hashtags; source line.
16. PROVEN (policy). Upload the clean original file to each platform; never re-upload another app's file.
17. IDEA. Posting frequency: 2-3 strong reels a day may beat 7 average ones (Buffer 2025, Mosseri "quality over
    volume"). Decision for Graziano; measure per-reel retention as volume grows.
18. IDEA. Trial Reels to A/B test hooks once the account has 1,000 followers.
19. IDEA. Trending audio on TikTok (added in-app by Graziano) and on a few Instagram reels, to compare.

## From competitor research (2026-10-23)
21. IDEA. A real person or real case as the hook ("A man held in a sneeze...") - strongest pattern in the
    niche (Zack D. Films, Karan Rajan).
22. IDEA. Test an AI voiceover on 2-3 reels: almost every top channel in the niche uses a narrator.
23. IDEA. A tiny source line on screen ("BMJ 2018") as an authority cue.
24. IDEA. Mild body horror and survival limits (no air, water, altitude) drive shares and comments; keep
    visuals non-gory.

## Truth
20. PROVEN (non-negotiable). Every claim true; hype the tone, not the facts. A source for every timer stop.

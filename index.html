Next Human Publisher

Next Human Publisher is a private tool used only by the creator of Next Human (@nnexthuman), a channel of short educational videos about the human body, longevity, AI and the future.

The tool publishes the creator's own videos, with their captions, to the creator's own accounts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok at scheduled times, and reads basic statistics about those posts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

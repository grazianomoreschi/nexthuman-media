Next Human Publisher · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

Next Human Publisher ("the app") is used only by the owner of the Next Human accounts (@nnexthuman) to publish their own videos.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts (account ID, name, handle).
  • The owner's own videos, captions and comments on those videos, and statistics about the owner's posts (views, likes, shares, saves, followers).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos, post the owner's captions and first comments, and show the owner statistics about their own content. The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data from other users.

Use of Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private cloud environment and are never published. Videos are hosted publicly only for the few minutes needed for the platform to download them, then removed.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions). After revocation the app stops accessing the account. To request deletion of any stored data, contact the owner via Instagram direct message to @nnexthuman.
